The challenge

PIPELINE is a multidisciplinary, Navy-funded engineering program serving students, researchers, university leadership, industry partners and government stakeholders. My job was to make technically complex work clear and useful to very different audiences without losing the substance behind it.

My role

  • Ran the marketing function as the program's sole marketer across web, social, email, print, events and recruitment
  • Worked with technical staff and leadership to translate program capabilities, research, projects and outcomes into public-facing communications
  • Built presentations and materials for sponsors, partners, program leadership and external stakeholders
  • Launched the first email newsletter and grew it from zero to 200+ subscribers across 20+ issues
  • Improved Craft CMS pages for SEO and accessibility, then used Google Analytics to guide digital decisions
  • Reported marketing performance for program leadership and annual reporting, including reporting sent to the Navy

Technical storytelling

I adjusted the level of technical detail and the message to what each audience needed to understand or do next, whether the audience was a prospective student, a faculty researcher, a program partner or leadership.
