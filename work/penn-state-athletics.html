Penn State Athletics.

I designed the social graphics and produced the short-form video for Penn State Altoona Athletics, from AMCC award and all-conference designs to game-day reels that became the program's most-watched posts.

The work

I covered the program across basketball, volleyball, and soccer, turning conference honors and game days into a consistent, on-brand social feed. The graphics carried the Penn State and AMCC identities; the video drove the reach.

  • Designed AMCC award graphics: player of the week, all-conference, and all-sportsmanship honors.
  • Built game-day and player-feature content across five sports.
  • Shot, produced, and edited short-form video, including the program's most-viewed reels.
