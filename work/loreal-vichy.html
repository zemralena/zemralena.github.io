L'Oréal × Vichy.

I led a creative team to build a 360° marketing campaign launching Vichy Dercos in the U.S., and presented our concepts directly to the SVP, Head of Brand at L'Oréal.

The work

The ask was to build awareness for Vichy Dercos in the U.S. by defining a premium value proposition and carrying one idea across social, retail, and experiential channels. We grounded the campaign in a real consumer insight, the distracted, self-conscious moments dandruff creates, and turned it into a confident creative platform.

  • Led the team through research, the consumer insight, and campaign architecture.
  • Built a full-funnel platform across editorial, social commerce, and experiential.
  • Developed executions: a Men's Health editorial feature, a TikTok Shop launch, and a La Roche-Posay × Dercos pop-up.
  • Presented the final concepts to the SVP, Head of Brand at L'Oréal.
