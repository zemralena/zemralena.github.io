## Results methodology

TEDx Instagram: 90-day and 30-day account Insights PDFs exported February 18, 2026. The UI retains the source's rolling-window labels. Reach is distinct accounts; views are not unique people. Non-follower percentage is the share of views, not follower growth.

TEDx LinkedIn: daily rows in the `Metrics` sheets, rows 3–92 (90 days) and 3–32 (30 days). The totals use columns D (impressions), H (clicks), K (reactions), N (comments), Q (reposts). The weighted engagement rate is the sum of clicks, reactions, comments and reposts divided by impressions. No average of daily percentages is used. The 30-day window is contained within the 90-day window; they are not presented as sequential periods.

- November 19, 2025–February 16, 2026: 11,281 impressions; 517 clicks; 244 reactions; 17 comments; 3 reposts; 6.923145% engagement rate.
- January 18–February 16, 2026: 5,027 impressions; 247 clicks; 114 reactions; 2 comments; 1 repost; 7.240899% engagement rate.

TEDx email: October 2025, November 2025, January 2026 and February 2026 Mailchimp reports. Rates are reported directly and reflect different observation windows as of February 18, 2026. Only aggregate metrics are published, not the admin exports or recipient information.

PIPELINE results: March–July 2025 counts from https://zemralenaportfolio.my.canva.site/analytics: 6,439 impressions; 249 reactions + 28 comments + 39 shares = 316 social engagements. The earlier differently sized window is omitted rather than presented as a like-for-like growth comparison. Unsupported headline claims from the prior V5 page are replaced by these labeled totals.

These are project-result visualizations. No visitor-tracking service or analytics account is configured.
